# Code of Conduct

Kasane contributors are expected to make technical collaboration safe,
professional, and welcoming. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews,
discussions, security reports, release coordination, and project events.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person presenting them.
- Be specific, evidence-led, and patient when explaining risks or requesting
  changes.
- Respect different backgrounds, identities, experience levels, languages,
  accessibility needs, and communication styles.
- Give credit, preserve authorship, and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.
- Protect private reports, personal information, credentials, and embargoed
  security details.
- Accept maintainer decisions and disengage when a discussion is closed.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, stalking, discrimination, sexualized attention, or
  demeaning language.
- Personal insults, deliberate intimidation, repeated disruption, or sustained
  off-topic advocacy.
- Publishing another person's private information or undisclosed vulnerability
  details without permission.
- Using project channels to solicit credentials, access, money, or unrelated
  commercial promotion.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Reporting and enforcement

For ordinary conduct concerns, contact the repository maintainers privately
through the hosting platform. Do not continue the dispute in a public thread.
For a concern that also exposes a vulnerability or sensitive technical detail,
use the
[private security reporting channel](./SECURITY.md#reporting-a-vulnerability).

Maintainers will limit disclosure to people needed to assess the report, give
the affected person an opportunity to provide context when safe, and choose a
proportionate response. Responses may include a private warning, content editing
or removal, temporary participation limits, removal from project spaces, or a
permanent ban. Immediate action may be taken to protect people or sensitive
information.

Maintainers with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves. Good-faith
reports and appeals will not be punished merely because an allegation cannot be
substantiated. Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves violate this
code.

## Scope and attribution

This code governs project-managed spaces and conduct by project representatives
when they are acting on behalf of Kasane. It is an original project policy and
may be revised through the normal documented governance process.
